## The short version

We collect only what we need to run your trip, we do not sell or share it for advertising, and this website sets no cookies and runs no ad trackers.

## What we collect

When you request or book a trip: your name, email, phone number, pickup location, trip date, group size and anything you choose to tell us (for example mobility needs or a celebration). If someone in your group needs a car seat, we note that too.

When you pay: payment is handled by our payment provider. We never receive or store full card numbers.

When you visit the site: no cookies are set. If we add visitor statistics, they will be cookieless and will not identify you.

## Why we use it

- to plan and run your trip, and to contact you about it
- to send one short message after your trip asking for a review, which you can ignore
- to keep records required by law, such as tax and business records

## Who we share it with

Only the people needed to run your trip: for example a tasting room, observatory or guided-tour company when we reserve on your behalf, our payment provider and our booking system. We do not sell your information.

## How long we keep it

Trip records for as long as tax and business record rules require, then we delete them. Ask us to delete anything we are not required to keep and we will.

## Your choices

Ask us at any time what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Contact details are on our [contact page](/contact/).

## Children

The site is not aimed at children. We only collect a child's information (such as a car-seat need) from the adult who books.
