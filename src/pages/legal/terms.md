These terms apply to every trip booked with Three Tickets to Adventure LLC ("we", "us"), an Arizona limited liability company based in Flagstaff. By confirming a booking you agree to them, to the [cancellation and weather policy](/legal/cancellation/) and, before your trip, to the [release and assumption of risk](/legal/release/).

## Booking and price

- A request is not a booking. A booking exists once we confirm it in writing and payment is received.
- Prices are per vehicle for the number of guests stated on the trip page and include Arizona transaction privilege tax.
- Costs listed on a trip page as "paid at cost" (for example park entry, tasting fees or admission tickets) are passed on without markup and are not included in the trip price.
- Payment is taken by our payment provider. We never see or store full card numbers.

## Who rides

- The person who books is responsible for everyone in their group following these terms.
- The number of guests may not exceed the number confirmed, and never more than 6.
- Children who need a car seat or booster seat under Arizona law must have one; tell us when you book.
- Everyone wears a seat belt whenever the car is moving.

## Conduct and alcohol

- Our driver never consumes alcohol or drugs before or during a trip. See our [zero-tolerance policy](/legal/safety/).
- Guests may not consume alcohol or drugs in the car or carry open containers. Sealed bottles bought on a trip ride in the cargo area.
- No smoking or vaping in the car.
- We may end a trip without refund if a guest's behavior puts anyone's safety at risk, damages the car, or breaks the law.

## Itineraries and changes

- Itineraries are a plan, not a guarantee. We may change the order of stops, shorten time at a stop or substitute a stop because of weather, traffic, closures, crowding or safety, without refund where the trip still runs substantially as described.
- Third-party venues (wineries, observatories, parks, restaurants, guided tours) set their own hours, rules and prices and are responsible for their own services.

## Pickup and waiting

- Please be ready at the confirmed pickup time and place. We wait 15 minutes; after that we try to reach you, and a trip that cannot start within 30 minutes of the confirmed time counts as a no-show.

## Damage

- Guests are responsible for damage to the car beyond normal wear caused by their group, including cleaning costs above normal.

## Liability

- Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited under Arizona law.
- To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for indirect or consequential losses, such as missed onward travel, or for the acts of third-party venues.

## Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Arizona. Any dispute will be heard in the courts of Coconino County, Arizona.
