## The car

Our car is a Toyota Highlander, an SUV with a step-in height of roughly knee height and no running boards. It is **not wheelchair accessible**. A folding wheelchair fits in the back when there are 3 or fewer guests.

If that does not work for you, please tell us. We are tracking how many guests need an accessible vehicle; it is the case for adding one.

## The trips

Each trip page has an "Access and comfort" table with walking surfaces, restrooms and altitude, so you can judge before you book rather than after. Where we have not yet measured a distance, the page says so.

Northern Arizona is high: Flagstaff and the Grand Canyon's South Rim are around 7,000 feet. If you have heart or breathing conditions, ask your doctor before booking.

## This website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA: readable color contrast, full keyboard use, descriptive labels and no content that depends on color alone. If anything on the site does not work for you, tell us through the [contact page](/contact/) and we will fix it.
