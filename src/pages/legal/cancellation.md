## If you cancel

| When you cancel | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| 72 hours or more before pickup | 100% |
| Less than 72 hours before pickup | 50% |
| No-show, or a trip that cannot start within 30 minutes of the confirmed time | None |

Changing to another date is free if you ask at least 72 hours before pickup and we have the new date available.

Costs we have already paid on your behalf to third parties that will not refund us (for example non-refundable admission or guided-tour tickets) are not refunded by us inside 72 hours.

## If we cancel

We cancel or move a trip when it would be unsafe or impossible to run, including:

- snow or ice on the route, or chain controls
- flash-flood, fire or high-wind warnings on the route
- road, park or venue closures, including a government shutdown
- the car being unsafe to drive, or the driver being unwell

When we cancel, we tell you as early as we can and you choose a **full refund** or a **new date**. We are not responsible for hotel, flight or other costs you booked separately.

## Weather calls

Northern Arizona weather changes fast: snow from late fall to spring, and afternoon monsoon storms in July and August. We watch the forecast and road conditions the day before and the morning of your trip, and we may move your start time to avoid a storm. If the trip still runs substantially as described, a weather-related change of timing or order is not a cancellation.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 10 business days of the cancellation.
