Every guest signs this release before the trip begins. A parent or legal guardian signs for each guest under 18.

## 1. The trip

I am taking part in a private sightseeing trip in Northern Arizona operated by Three Tickets to Adventure LLC ("the Company"), which may include travel by car on highways and mountain roads, short walks at viewpoints and visits to third-party venues.

## 2. Risks I understand

I understand that the trip involves risks that cannot be removed entirely, including:

- traffic collisions and the actions of other drivers
- uneven, steep, icy or unfenced ground, cliff edges and stairs at viewpoints
- high altitude (up to about 7,000 feet or more), heat, cold, sun, storms, lightning and flash floods
- wildlife, insects and plants
- remote areas with no phone signal, far from medical care
- the acts of third-party venues and their staff

## 3. My responsibilities

- I have told the Company about any medical condition, allergy or mobility limit that could affect my safety, and I am fit to take part.
- I will wear a seat belt, follow the driver's safety instructions, stay behind railings and on marked paths, and carry water and the clothing recommended for the trip.
- I will not consume alcohol or drugs in the vehicle.

## 4. Release

To the fullest extent permitted by Arizona law, I voluntarily assume the risks described above and release the Company, its members and its drivers from claims for injury, loss or damage arising from those inherent risks. This release does not apply to injury caused by the Company's gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

## 5. Medical care

I authorize the Company to seek emergency medical care for me if I am unable to consent, at my own cost.

## 6. Photographs

The Company may photograph the trip and use images in which I appear for marketing, unless I opt out in writing before the trip.

## 7. Severability

If any part of this release is found unenforceable, the rest remains in force.
