## Zero-tolerance drug and alcohol policy

Three Tickets to Adventure LLC has a zero-tolerance policy on the use of drugs and alcohol by any driver while providing service, as required of every vehicle-for-hire company in Arizona (A.R.S. § 28-9507).

- No driver may drive for us after consuming alcohol, cannabis or any impairing drug, or while impaired by any medication.
- No driver may carry open alcohol or illegal drugs in the vehicle.
- A driver who breaks this policy is removed from service immediately and permanently.

## Complaints and concerns

### If you suspect a driver is impaired

1. If you feel unsafe, ask the driver to stop in a safe place and end the trip. Call 911 in an emergency.
2. Report it to us as soon as you can by phone or email, listed on our [contact page](/contact/), with the date, time and location.
3. When we receive a complaint, the driver is suspended from driving immediately while we investigate. We reply to you in writing with what we found and what we did.
4. You may also report a vehicle-for-hire concern to the Arizona Department of Transportation, Motor Vehicle Division.

### Anything else

Anything else about a trip, a driver or the car: tell us the same way. We answer every complaint in writing.

## The vehicle

- Brakes and tires are inspected by a qualified mechanic at least once a year, and we keep maintenance records for two years.
- Open safety recalls are completed before the car carries guests.
- Winter tires are fitted in the snow season; we do not drive unpaved Forest roads.

## Drivers

- Every driver passes a criminal background check before driving for us, and holds a valid Arizona driver license.
- Drivers keep a first aid kit and water in the car.

## Insurance and permits

We list our permits and insurance on our [About page](/about/) and in the site footer once each is in place. We only sell trips we are permitted to run.
