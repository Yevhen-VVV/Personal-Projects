The Verde Valley grows wine at the foot of the Mogollon Rim, and most visitors only learn that once they are here. The problem is always the same: someone has to drive. On this day nobody does except us.

We book the tasting rooms ahead, keep the pace easy and leave room for lunch. Jerome comes last, when the light on the valley below is at its best.

Tell us what you like to drink when you book and we will pick tasting rooms to match.
