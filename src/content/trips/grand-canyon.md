Most people see the Grand Canyon from the rim behind a crowd, on someone else's schedule. This day is built the other way around: we leave when the light will be good where we are going, skip the stops that are only crowded, and stay longer at the ones that make you go quiet.

The drive up from Flagstaff crosses ponderosa forest and open high desert, and it is part of the day, not dead time. Ask anything along the way.

Choose the daytime version for the most viewpoints, or the sunset version for the canyon turning red and then purple, followed by a dark drive home under the stars.
