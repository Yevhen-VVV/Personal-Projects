Flagstaff sits in the middle of a landscape most visitors drive straight past on the way to the Grand Canyon: a young volcano, the stone pueblos of the people who farmed its ash, cliff dwellings in a hidden canyon and a crater left by a falling iron meteorite.

All four are close to town, which makes this an easy, varied day without long drives.
