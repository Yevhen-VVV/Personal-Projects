Sedona's problem is not the scenery. It is the traffic, the full parking lots and the time spent looking for a space. On this trip the car is the best seat in the house: you watch Oak Creek Canyon unwind on the way down, and we drop you at the door of each stop.

This trip is not bookable yet. We are confirming the route with the Forest Service first, because selling a trip before it is cleared is not something we do.
