Phoenix is where most people land, and the two-hour climb from the Sonoran Desert to the red rocks or the pines is a better first chapter than a shuttle van. You ride privately, with room to stretch out, and stop once where the view opens up.

This trip opens once our Sky Harbor permit is issued.
