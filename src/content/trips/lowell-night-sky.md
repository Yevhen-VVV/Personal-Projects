Flagstaff was the world's first International Dark Sky City, and Lowell Observatory is why. On a clear night the Milky Way is visible from town, and Lowell's telescopes bring the planets close.

We pick you up, make sure your admission is sorted and bring you back afterwards. You spend the evening looking up instead of reading road signs in the dark.
