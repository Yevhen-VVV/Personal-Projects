# Domain 8: Software Development Security (10% of the exam)

## D8-001 | 8.1 | 1
Q: In which development methodology does each phase finish before the next begins, with little provision for going back?
A)* Waterfall
= Correct. Waterfall is sequential; changes discovered late are expensive.
B) Agile with two-week sprints
= Agile delivers in short iterations and expects change.
C) The spiral model with repeated risk analysis
= Spiral iterates through planning, risk analysis, engineering and evaluation.
D) DevOps with continuous delivery
= DevOps integrates development and operations for frequent, continuous releases.
>> Waterfall: sequential. Spiral: iterative and risk-driven. Agile: iterative and adaptive. DevOps: continuous delivery.
